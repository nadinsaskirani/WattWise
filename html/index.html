Why is it important?

Household

Lighter bills

Appliances that use a lot of electricity show up directly in your monthly bill.

Environment

Lower emissions

Electricity you do not use means fuel that does not have to be burned.

Cities

A healthier Earth

Saving energy supports climate action and sustainable cities.
