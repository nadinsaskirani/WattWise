Why do we need to save energy?

Electricity feels clean when we flip the switch, but behind it is a generation process that affects the environment. Most of Indonesia's electricity is still produced by fossil-fuel power plants such as coal.

What is the link between electricity and global warming?

  1. We use electricity for daily activities, from turning on the lights to watching television.
  2. To supply that electricity, fossil-fuel power plants burn fuels such as coal.
  3. This burning releases greenhouse gases, including carbon dioxide, into the atmosphere.
  4. Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere, so the Earth gets warmer and the climate changes.

That is why every bit of electricity we save helps ease the load on this chain.

Impact on health

Wasting electricity often starts with small habits we do not notice, such as leaving the lights on in an empty room or keeping the television on standby. Even though they look like they are not in use, devices in this mode still consume electricity. If this goes on, unnecessary electricity use can add to the monthly bill.

Impact of energy on the environment

  • Power plants that still use coal and other fossil fuels produce greenhouse gas emissions, mainly carbon dioxide, which contribute to climate change.
  • The more electricity households consume, the more energy power plants need to produce.
  • Wasteful energy use also increases air pollution and puts pressure on natural resources.

Buildings account for about 30% of global energy demand. Of that amount, about 70% is used by the household sector. Source: International Energy Agency (IEA), Energy Efficiency 2025 Buildings.

Who benefits?

Families

Lower electricity bills, and the savings can go toward other needs.

Environment

Fewer emissions and cleaner air.

Country

Energy supply needs are easier to manage, so more people can be served.

Which appliances usually use the most power?

Appliances that produce heat or cold tend to use the most electricity, such as air conditioners, water heaters, irons, and old refrigerators. Check the power label on your appliances to compare them.

ApplianceEstimated power
1 PK air conditionerabout 600–900 watts

Power varies by brand and model, so the figures above are only estimates. Sources: CNBC Indonesia (2026), Metro TV (2025), and JournalArta (2026).

Link to the SDGs

SDG 7 Affordable and clean energy

This goal ensures access to affordable, reliable, and sustainable energy for everyone. Reducing energy waste is a small step that supports it.

SDG 12 Responsible consumption and production

Using energy only as much as we need is a form of responsible consumption.

SDG 13 Climate action

Using less electricity from fossil fuels helps reduce emissions.

What can we do?

Start with small things at home, then invite family and friends. When many people do it, the impact is big. See how on the Saving Tips and Green Home pages.
