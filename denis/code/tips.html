Saving Tips

Energy-saving tips for your home

Every tip below supports one of the SDG goals.

Start the checklist

💡 Switch to LED lights

They use far less electricity than incandescent bulbs and last much longer.

SDG 7

❄️ Set your AC to 24-25°C

A temperature that is too low makes the compressor work harder.

SDG 13

🔌 Unplug chargers and unused devices

Devices that stay plugged in still draw standby power.

SDG 12

🌿 Use natural light and airflow

Open the windows during the day and plant shade trees around your home.

SDG 11

⭐ Choose energy-efficient appliances

Check the star label on ACs, refrigerators, and washing machines before you buy.
