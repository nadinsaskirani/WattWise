What are the SDGs?

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are 17 global goals set by the United Nations, to be achieved by 2030. They aim to end poverty, protect the planet, and make sure everyone can live well.

7

Affordable and Clean Energy

Making sure everyone has access to energy that is reliable, modern, affordable, and sustainable. This is the focus of the Wattwise website.

Five targets of SDG 7

Click each target to read a short explanation.

7.1 Energy access for all

Everyone, including people in remote areas, can get electricity and clean cooking fuel at an affordable price.

7.2 More renewable energy

A growing share of electricity comes from solar, water, wind, and geothermal sources. See examples on the renewable energy page.

7.3 Energy efficiency

Appliances and buildings use less energy to do the same job. At home, this means choosing efficient appliances and changing daily habits.

7.a Clean energy cooperation

Countries work together on research, technology, and investment in clean energy.

7.b Infrastructure and technology

Building networks and technology so that modern energy reaches developing countries.
